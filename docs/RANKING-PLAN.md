# Plan to outrank the competition

Revised **2 August 2026**, after the owner confirmed the full product specification in [PRODUCT-SPECS.md](PRODUCT-SPECS.md). Competitor and search-results evidence is in [profile.md](profile.md), verified 26–27 July 2026.

**The strategic reading in one paragraph.** There is only one strong Cypriot competitor — Hercules Group in Limassol — and it publishes **nothing in Greek**. The one rival with a real audience in our own neighbourhood, S&H Laser Signs, has 3,690 Instagram followers pointing at a **67-word** flags page. Two Kaimakli names are entirely offline. The original plan was to beat them on depth and Greek-language coverage. The specification answers changed that: we can now beat them on **speed and completeness**, which is far harder to copy. **The position is: the Cypriot workshop that has it in stock, makes the bespoke work in half the time, and specifies every part of it.** Do not fight Greek e-commerce on commodity national flags, where they are strongest and we are weakest.

---

## The competitive advantages, in priority order

Each of these is confirmed and stateable. The first two are the ones no competitor can answer.

1. **Stock and same-day collection.** Standard sizes are held; 1–5 days if out. Hercules publishes **21 days for everything**, and no other Cypriot supplier states a turnaround at all. A buyer who needs a flag for a ceremony next week currently has no visible option in Cyprus.
2. **Ten days for custom prints, against a published 21.** Less than half the market benchmark, from the only competitor who publishes a number.
3. **The λάβαρο specified end to end** — velvet in three colours, appliqué lettering, printed or embroidered emblem, gold fringing, tassels in any colour, aluminium pole 39 mm × 3 m at 2–2.5 kg with an optional split, PVC cross or bronze πόμωλο, cow-leather harness with two adjustable straps. Nobody in Cyprus or Greece publishes anything approaching this. It reads as a workshop that makes the thing rather than a shop that orders it in.
4. **Repairs and restoration of old λάβαρα.** Zero competitors advertise it anywhere. Cheap traffic, high intent, and repair customers commission new work later.
5. **No minimum order.** Sapo demands 300 pieces; nobody else states a minimum at all.
6. **Poles and fittings sold separately.** Heavy and expensive to ship from Greece — a structural local moat.
7. **Local manufacture.** A real factory in Kaimakli, not an importer. Samples to view, and the person who answers the phone is the person who makes it.
8. **The giant flag** — 58 × 27 m, fifteen rolls of fabric, three days' work, too large to unroll indoors. Unrepeatable by any competitor.

**What we do not lead with:** fabric weight (120 gsm is *below* Hercules' published 140), single-sided show-through printing, and anything resembling a rush service — the owner asked that rush orders never be mentioned.

## The cost of publishing no prices

Settled and not up for revisiting, but the trade-off should be understood rather than forgotten. Without prices we cannot earn Google's product rich results or merchant listings, and `τιμή`/`τιμές` appear in a large share of buying queries.

The mitigation is to answer the price question **without numbers**: a "what determines the cost of a λάβαρο" section — size, velvet colour, embroidered versus printed emblem, fringing, pole, harness — captures the query, satisfies the intent and routes to a phone call. A partial recovery, not a full one.

## Phase 4 — Fix the signals feeding all of it

The site is the fastest and best-structured in the market, but **nothing feeds it**.

1. **Reviews: 14 → 50+.** Hercules has 192, Laser Signs 60. The single biggest ranking factor still available. Steer people to the **store** profile — the factory profile has one review. See [REVIEWS.md](REVIEWS.md).
2. **Restart social** — Facebook silent since **March 2023**; Instagram has **76 followers** against Laser Signs' 3,690. See [SOCIAL.md](SOCIAL.md).
3. **Google Business Profiles** — add the store's opening hours, change its category from the generic "Store", add photos to both. *(Both already point correctly at chrossides.com and are rated 4.6 and 5.0.)*
4. **Directory listings** — see [OFFSITE.md](OFFSITE.md). **Sapo and Pafitis are both listed under Flags & Banners but sell no flags**; their directory traffic is takeable.

## How we will know it is working

Track in Search Console (Performance → Queries), filtered to Cyprus, not by guessing from your own browser:

- Impressions and average position for the target Greek queries above.
- Clicks on `/proionta/` pages versus the homepage.
- **Local pack visibility** — searches for "σημαίες Λευκωσία" from a phone in Nicosia. The US-based tooling used for this research cannot see the local pack, and for these queries it is probably the most important surface of all.

Reasonable expectation: gap-page rankings within **2–3 months** given how weak the competition is on those terms. Commodity national-flag queries may never be won, and that is fine — they are the wrong customers. Note that queries like "flags cyprus" are read by Google as *the national flag of Cyprus*, an informational query dominated by Wikipedia and stock-photo sites; they are not a useful benchmark for this business.
