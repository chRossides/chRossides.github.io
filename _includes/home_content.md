


We excel in the meticulous crafting of custom-made flags, banners, embroidery, and army-related products. This family-owned enterprise is renowned for its superior quality, producing bespoke printed and sewn items specifically tailored to meet the unique needs of each client. With a storied heritage in creating distinctive and durable products, we proudly maintain a tradition of unparalleled craftsmanship.

#### Our Custom Offerings

- **Flags**: Whether national or custom-designed with your company logo, we create high-quality flags to suit your specifications.
- **Kepis**: We manufacture kepis for police and military use, as well as custom designs.
- **Embroidery Badges**: Specializing in embroidery for police and military badges, we also offer custom designs.
- **Military Accessories**: From name tags and badges to hats, boots, shirts, and equipment, we provide a comprehensive range of military accessories.
- **Parade Banners**: Our parade banners can be customized to include or exclude the pole, sheathed in velvet or not, topped with a white sphere or a brass cross, and available with single or two-piece poles.
- **Hunting Clothing and Accessories**: We also supply a variety of hunting clothing and accessories to meet your needs.
