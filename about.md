Established in 1938, Charalambos Rossides LTD is a distinguished family-owned enterprise based in Nicosia, Cyprus. With over eight decades of experience, the company has honed its expertise in producing high-quality custom-made flags, banners, embroidery, and army-related products.

From its inception, Charalambos Rossides LTD has been committed to delivering exceptional craftsmanship. The company prides itself on its ability to create bespoke items tailored to the unique needs of its clients, ensuring top-notch quality and customer satisfaction.

Throughout its history, the company has successfully adapted to changing market demands while maintaining its core values of quality and precision. The dedication to excellence has allowed Charalambos Rossides LTD to build a loyal client base.

Today, the company continues to thrive under the management of the founder's descendants, who uphold the tradition of quality and innovation that has defined Charalambos Rossides LTD for generations.
